## Full job statements

### 1. `modernize-without-a-reset` ★
**When** another upgrade cycle forces a disruptive migration and the board is asking why AI is stalled, **I want to** modernize onto a platform that keeps evolving without rip-and-replace, **so I can** support the business's ambitions without downtime or risking my credibility.
- **Functional:** scale/refresh without migration
- **Emotional:** stop fearing the next reset
- **Social:** be the leader who future-proofed us
- Content it claims: Evergreen/upgrade content, modernization case studies

### 2. `survive-a-ransomware-attack` ★
**When** ransomware is hitting my industry and our backups themselves are a target, **I want** recovery I can trust to be clean and fast even with compromised admin credentials, **so I can** remove the attackers' leverage and never choose between paying and weeks of downtime.
- **Functional:** immutable copies; clean restore in minutes–hours
- **Emotional:** sleep through the threat-intel headlines
- **Social:** be the one who was ready, not the cautionary tale
- Content it claims: SafeMode / cyber-resilience / recovery content

### 3. `keep-the-business-always-on`
**When** even minutes of downtime cost revenue and reputation and maintenance windows have shrunk to nothing, **I want** operations to continue through failures, upgrades, and site loss, **so I can** meet always-on expectations without heroics.
- **Functional:** HA, replication, zero-RPO failover
- **Emotional:** stop dreading maintenance windows and 2 a.m. pages
- **Social:** own five-nines credibly
- Content it claims: ActiveCluster / DR / business-continuity content

### 4. `govern-data-for-ai` ★
**When** data use grows faster than our governance, **I want** sensitive data classified, protected, and accessible, **so I can** enable AI without increasing risk.
- **Functional:** classification, encryption, residency, policy
- **Emotional:** confidence saying yes to AI
- **Social:** the enabler, not the department of no
- Content it claims: governance / sovereignty / compliance-for-AI content

### 5. `keep-the-gpus-fed` ★
**When** expensive AI infrastructure sits idle waiting on data, **I want** storage that feeds training and inference without bottlenecks, **so I can** get ROI from the AI investment.
- **Functional:** sustained GPU utilization, pipeline throughput
- **Emotional:** end the "storage is the bottleneck" accusations
- **Social:** the platform team that keeps AI moving
- Content it claims: AI-infrastructure / throughput / benchmark content

### 6. `get-ai-from-pilot-to-production`
**When** AI pilots prove value but stall before production because the infrastructure can't scale them, **I want** a repeatable path to industrialize AI workloads, **so I can** show ROI instead of a graveyard of pilots.
- **Functional:** scale pilots to production reliably
- **Emotional:** relief from board pressure
- **Social:** the executive who delivered AI, not just talked about it
- Content it claims: AI-strategy / maturity / ROI content

### 7. `de-risk-the-vendor-switch` ★
**When** I've decided the incumbent can't stretch further, **I want to** change vendors without a painful cutover, **so I can** move forward without betting my quarter on it.
- **Functional:** proven migration path, references, PoC
- **Emotional:** confidence in the leap
- **Social:** a switch that makes me look smart, not reckless
- Content it claims: comparison / migration-proof / security-validation content

### 8. `escape-hypervisor-lock-in`
**When** licensing changes raise my costs faster than my value and lock-in dictates my roadmap, **I want to** re-platform virtualization on my timeline with a reversible path, **so I can** control my own costs and future.
- **Functional:** credible alternative path without disruption
- **Emotional:** regain control
- **Social:** decisive, not captive
- Content it claims: the VMware-era virtualization/migration content

### 9. `cut-power-space-and-carbon`
**When** energy costs and sustainability mandates turn the data center into a liability, **I want** dramatic footprint reduction without performance trade-offs, **so I can** hit ESG and budget targets while freeing capacity for growth.
- **Functional:** more capability per watt and rack-U
- **Emotional:** off the back foot on energy
- **Social:** credible on ESG to the board
- Content it claims: sustainability / efficiency content

### 10. `do-more-with-a-flat-team`
**When** manual operations consume my team and headcount won't grow, **I want** operations automated and self-service, **so I can** redirect people to work that moves the business.
- **Functional:** automation, APIs, self-service catalogs
- **Emotional:** end the firefighting and burnout
- **Social:** run a modern, strategic shop
- Content it claims: automation / fleet-management / AIOps content

### 11. `consolidate-the-sprawl`
**When** acquisitions and point solutions have left data fragmented across silos and tools, **I want to** consolidate onto fewer, simpler platforms, **so I can** cut cost and complexity and finally see everything in one place.
- **Functional:** unify workloads, protocols, management
- **Emotional:** end the whack-a-mole
- **Social:** the architect who brought order
- Content it claims: consolidation case studies, unified-platform content

### 12. `make-storage-costs-predictable`
**When** lumpy CapEx refreshes and over-provisioning break financial planning, **I want** spend aligned to actual consumption with predictable growth, **so I can** plan budgets confidently and pay only for what we use.
- **Functional:** consumption model, right-sizing
- **Emotional:** no budget-surprise dread
- **Social:** CFO-credible stewardship
- Content it claims: STaaS / economics / TCO content

### 13. `guarantee-critical-app-performance`
**When** a revenue- or care-critical application slows and users feel it, **I want to** restore performance headroom and keep latency predictable as load grows, **so I can** protect the experience the business depends on.
- **Functional:** latency/IOPS/throughput headroom
- **Emotional:** stop being the blame target
- **Social:** trusted keeper of critical systems
- Content it claims: database / EHR / EDA performance content

### 14. `run-stateful-apps-on-kubernetes`
**When** applications move to containers but the data layer lacks enterprise guarantees, **I want** cloud-native data services as dependable as the old stack, **so I can** modernize applications without trading away resilience.
- **Functional:** persistent storage, HA/DR/backup for K8s
- **Emotional:** confidence in the new stack
- **Social:** platform-team credibility with app teams
- Content it claims: Portworx / containers / stateful-apps content

### 15. `operate-data-across-clouds`
**When** workloads span on-prem and multiple clouds, each with its own tools, **I want** one consistent way to run, move, and protect data anywhere, **so I can** place workloads on merit instead of by lock-in.
- **Functional:** consistent APIs, data mobility
- **Emotional:** freedom from feeling stuck
- **Social:** strategic architect, not vendor-captive
- Content it claims: hybrid / multicloud content

### 16. `absorb-relentless-data-growth`
**When** data grows 20–30% a year and every scaling step means pain today, **I want** capacity and performance that scale in place without re-architecting, **so I can** say yes to growth instead of rationing it.
- **Functional:** TB→PB in one namespace, no forklift
- **Emotional:** out of scarcity mode
- **Social:** the enabler, not the gatekeeper
- Content it claims: genomics / media / unstructured-growth content

### 17. `make-a-defensible-technology-choice`
**When** I must form a view on an unfamiliar technology quickly, **I want** genuine fluency in the options and trade-offs, **so I can** recommend with confidence and defend it under scrutiny.
- **Functional:** understand concepts and trade-offs
- **Emotional:** confidence, no impostor feeling
- **Social:** the credible expert in the room
- Content it claims: **the 389-page glossary library**, explainers, comparisons

### 18. `build-a-practice-on-the-platform`
**When** choosing which vendor to invest my practice and portfolio in, **I want** a platform my business can differentiate on and operate profitably, **so I can** grow revenue without betting on the wrong horse.
- **Functional:** margin, enablement, market demand
- **Emotional:** confidence in the bet
- **Social:** valued advisor to my clients
- Content it claims: **the 193 partner pages**, program/enablement content

## Design notes
- **#2 / #3 split** the starter "recover with confidence": the adversarial circumstance (poisoned backups, clean-restore doubt) carries different anxieties than the outage/continuity circumstance.
- **#17 and #18** exist so the glossary and partner libraries classify honestly instead of force-fitting.
- Every statement is product-free and circumstance-anchored per the skill's non-negotiables.
- Once approved, each job becomes a `job-statement` object in `src/config/jtbdCatalog.json` with a stable `job.<slug>` id, and the slugs register as an enum-locked taxonomy dimension.
